Privacy Policy

AdMob Revenue Analytics · Effective date: September 1, 2025

01 Overview

AdWallet is a mobile analytics application that connects to your Google AdMob account and presents your ad revenue data in a clear, easy-to-read format. This Privacy Policy describes what information AdWallet accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your data.

Short version: AdWallet reads your AdMob reporting data to show it to you. It never writes to your account, never sells your data, and stores everything locally on your device.

02 Data We Collect

AdWallet only accesses information necessary to deliver its analytics features:

  • Google account credentials — your OAuth access token and profile information (display name, email address, profile photo) obtained when you sign in with Google. These are stored locally on your device in the system Keychain.
  • AdMob reporting data — revenue figures, impression counts, eCPM, and related metrics retrieved from the Google AdMob API. This data is fetched from Google's servers and displayed in-app; it is not transmitted to any server operated by AdWallet.
  • App preferences — settings such as your preferred currency and notification preferences, stored locally via AsyncStorage.
  • Purchase receipts — if you purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app upgrade, Google Play provides a purchase token used solely to verify and restore your entitlement. We do not store payment card details.

03 How We Use Your Data

All collected information is used exclusively to operate the app:

  • Authenticate and maintain your signed-in session with Google.
  • Fetch and display your AdMob revenue metrics and reports.
  • Remember your in-app preferences across sessions.
  • Verify and restore your "Remove Ads" purchase status.
  • Deliver optional local notifications about your daily revenue (only if you enable this feature).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating AdWallet for you.

04 Local Storage & Security

AdWallet is designed as a local-first application. Your credentials and settings never leave your device through AdWallet's own infrastructure:

  • Keychain — OAuth tokens are stored in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain, which is encrypted at the OS level and inaccessible to other apps.
  • AsyncStorage — app preferences are stored in the device's local storage.
  • No AdWallet backend — we do not operate a server that stores your personal data. All API calls are made directly from your device to Google's servers.

05 Third-Party Services

AdWallet integrates with the following external services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — used for authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob API — provides your revenue reporting data. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy. AdWallet requests read-only access; it cannot create, modify, or delete any AdMob resource.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Notifee — an open-source library used to schedule and display local notifications entirely on-device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

06 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Your explicit direction — for example, if you use an OS-level share feature within the app.
  • Legal obligation — if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.

We have no advertising partners, analytics SDKs that collect personal data, or data brokers involved in the operation of AdWallet.

07 Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you are in full control of its lifecycle:

  • Sign out — removes your stored OAuth tokens and clears your session immediately.
  • Uninstall the app — removes all locally stored data, including settings and cached tokens.
  • Revoke access — you can revoke AdWallet's Google account access at any time via Google Account Permissions.

We do not retain copies of your data on external servers, so there is no additional deletion step required on our end.

08 Children's Privacy

AdWallet is designed for AdMob publishers — individuals and organisations who monetise apps or websites through Google AdMob. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address the situation.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notice. Continued use of AdWallet after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
